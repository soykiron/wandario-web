Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. What data we collect

Your email address (for your account and to send you sign-in codes), your username, your profile photo if you upload one, your language, and whatever you mark inside the app: countries, regions, cities, villages, airports, your "Want to go" list, itineraries with their dates and notes, and your achievements. If you report or block another user, we store that report or block. We do not collect your location, we do not show ads and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

2. What we use it for

To make the app work (saving your progress, sending you sign-in codes, showing your profile to other users if you make it public), to review reports and keep the community safe, and to improve the service.

3. Who it is shared with

We do not sell your data. We use Supabase as our provider for database, accounts and photo storage, and Resend to send emails with your codes. To draw the map, your device requests map data from an open map server (OpenFreeMap), which therefore receives your IP address. Wandario PRO purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play: we do not receive or see your payment details, and to verify your purchases we may use a purchase-management service (RevenueCat). If you open a partner brand's website from the Traveler's Kit, you leave Wandario and that brand's privacy policy applies.

4. Your rights

You can access your data, correct it, delete it, object to its processing, restrict it or ask for portability. You can delete your account and all your data at any time from within the app, in Settings → Edit profile → Delete account: deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also exercise your rights by writing to hola@wandario.es. If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

5. Minors

Wandario is not aimed at people under 16. If we detect data from someone under that age, we delete it immediately.

6. Public profiles and blocks

By default, your username, your photo, your visited countries and your achievements are visible to other users of the app (this is how the Friends feature works). You can set your profile to private in Settings at any time, and you can block any user: you will no longer see or find each other.

7. Retention

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete it, it is erased. User reports may be kept anonymously for as long as needed for the safety of the community.

8. International data transfers

Your data may be stored or processed on servers outside your country of residence, always under the security safeguards of our providers.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will let you know about important changes inside the app.

11. Contact

The controller of your data is Iván Martínez Niño, owner of Wandario as a self-employed professional. For any privacy questions, write to us at hola@wandario.es.
